What does it cost?

Nothing. Pagemates is free.

What if I fall behind on the reading?

Come anyway. Discussions follow the pace in the club card, and spoilers stay out of the early meetings.

Who will be in my club?

Readers who chose the same book and the same time slot. Clubs stay small, 4 to 6 people, so everyone gets to talk.

Where do in-person clubs meet?

At public places readers suggest: cafés, bookstores, libraries. In-person clubs open in a city once enough readers there sign up. Until then we'll start you on video and tell you when your city opens.

Pagemates is in early access. Clubs open as enough readers join.
